# CoreBar Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026

CoreBar does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

CoreBar runs locally on your Mac and displays system usage information such as CPU, memory, disk usage, and current network transfer speed. Network speed is calculated from local interface byte counters; CoreBar does not inspect network content, addresses, domains, or connection history.

CoreBar does not require an account, does not include analytics, does not track users, and does not use third-party advertising services.

Because CoreBar does not collect or retain user data, there is no user data to delete and no data-collection consent to revoke. CoreBar does not share data with third parties.

If you have questions, contact the developer through the support page:

https://github.com/MightyKartz/CoreBar/issues
